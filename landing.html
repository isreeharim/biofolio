FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We’ve got answers.

Is Biofolio really 100% free?
Yes! Biofolio V1 is completely free. You get full access to the visual builder, custom themes, unlimited links, project showcases, and analytics without paying a penny.
Do I need to know how to code?
Not at all. Biofolio is 100% no-code. If you can type text and drag items on a screen, you can create a gorgeous portfolio in 5 minutes.
What will my portfolio URL look like?
Your portfolio will be available at biofolio.site/yourname. You can claim any available username when you sign up.
Can I add custom projects with images and tags?
Yes! You can add featured projects, case studies, images, tech stack tags, and external link buttons to showcase your real work.
How does analytics tracking work?
We track total page views and link clicks directly in your private dashboard. No invasive third-party tracking scripts or privacy-invading cookie banners.
